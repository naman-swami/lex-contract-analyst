# Legal Contract Clause Taxonomy

| Clause Classification | Typical Risk Level | Key Audit Inquiries |
|---|---|---|
| **Indemnification** | HIGH | Is indemnity mutual or unilateral? Are damages capped at contract value? |
| **Limitation of Liability** | HIGH | Does the agreement exclude consequential, indirect, or punitive damages? |
| **Termination for Convenience** | MEDIUM | What notice duration is required? Are sunk project costs reimbursed? |
| **Governing Law** | LOW | Which state or national jurisdiction governs contractual dispute resolution? |
